## Background Notes

This aircraft is the free Mini-family aircraft that was initially believed to have been interrupted during an update. Later physical inspection suggested an impact event instead.

Working theory: the aircraft was flown in sport mode, the pilot lost orientation, impacted nose-first, and cartwheeled. The impact pulled gimbal / arm mounting hardware and caused visible mechanical damage.
