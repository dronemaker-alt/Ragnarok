## Saltwater Risk Assessment

Saltwater exposure puts the following at high risk:

- ESC / power board
- Battery terminals
- Motor windings and bearings
- Board-to-board connectors
- Gimbal ribbon cable connectors
- Camera / gimbal board
- Battery BMS if battery was submerged

The airframe may still be valuable even if electronics are donor-only.

## Corrosion Recovery Procedure

1. Photograph aircraft before cleaning.
2. Remove battery and do not reuse until inspected separately.
3. Disassemble enough to expose affected boards.
4. Photograph boards before cleaning.
5. Rinse salt-contaminated boards with distilled water where appropriate.
6. Follow with 99% IPA.
7. Dry thoroughly.
8. Inspect under microscope.
9. Check resistance from main battery positive to ground.
10. Power only through current-limited supply or protected adapter.
